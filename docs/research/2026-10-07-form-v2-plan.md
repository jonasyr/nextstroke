# Form mode v2: finding forms with one tap (plan and pre-registration, 2026-10-07)

**Question (owner, 2026-10-07):** Make form mode work on most drawings, even if it is complicated and takes up to about a second, without overfitting to our own pictures.

**Approach under test (no model):** separate outline from hatching (short, dense strokes are not walls), close gaps by joining open line ends instead of thickening every line, merge parts across short inner lines so one tap finds the whole form, smoother inflation without pillow edges, and a round/flat suggestion from the outline. Budget: up to about one second per tap on a phone, with a visible "searching" state.

## Guarding against overfitting

- **Two sets, split at random before any development** (seed 20261007): 38 open drawings from the internet (Openverse/Flickr under CC licences, Cleveland Museum of Art CC0), used for testing only with the owner's approval and never committed. 15 form the **test set**, 23 plus the 13 Phase 0 drawings the **development set**.
- **Taps and expectations written before development**, one tap per object: `area` (a clearly outlined form: the right area expected), `refuse` (background: no area), `either` (unclear outline: right area or refusal are fine, a wrong area is not). Test set: 16 area, 11 refuse, 7 either. Development set: 18 area, 8 refuse, 15 either.
- **The test set is sealed:** SHA-256 of its taps and the split: `fc442e41bdfe9bf0c66a5366d2c1df9611e25e0373dcd197e834429b90e026cb`. Development looks only at the development set; the test set runs once, at the end, with v1 alongside for comparison.
- **No per-image rules:** every threshold scales with the image size or is a property of strokes (length, width, density).

## Criteria for the test set, fixed now

1. `area` taps: at least 70 % give the right area (covers most of the form as a person would outline it, spills only a thin margin).
2. `refuse` taps: at least 90 % refused.
3. Wrong areas over all taps: at most 10 %.
4. Shading with the labelled kind (round or flat) plausible for at least 70 % of right areas.
5. At most 1,000 ms per tap at 768 px in Node (95th percentile).
6. Reported, not gating: how often the round/flat suggestion matches the label.

**GO:** all of 1–5 hold; v2 replaces v1 in the app (D-074). **Otherwise:** v1 stays, the result is recorded here.
